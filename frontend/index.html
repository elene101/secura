    How it works

    From your first scan to your first alert.

    1. 01

      Enter an address

      Just type example.ge. No account is needed to scan.

    2. 02

      Read the result

      We check TLS, browser security headers and email DNS settings, and show how long the server took to respond.

    3. 03

      Add to monitoring

      Add your site to monitor it every 30 minutes. Verify the domain to choose a shorter interval.

    4. 04

      Get the alerts

      If a site goes down, we send an email, and an SMS in paid plans, and again when it recovers.
