Methodology

Monitors check an HTTP or HTTPS address. The default interval is 30 minutes. Verified domains can use 5- or 15-minute intervals. Availability is calculated from completed scheduled checks.

New failures are rechecked. The dashboard shows maintenance periods, overdue checks and incident history. A second region can confirm failures when a regional probe is configured.

The technical score reflects observed TLS, HTTP headers and related configuration. Response time is shown separately. The score is normalized over applicable measured checks. Unknown results reduce coverage.

DKIM detection checks common selectors. Domain expiry depends on the registry data available through RDAP or WHOIS. The optional reputation check uses Cloudflare for domains and Google Cloud Web Risk for individual URLs when configured.

Privacy

Secura GE uses account information, monitored URLs and contact details to provide monitoring and alerts. Email and SMS providers process the information needed to send those notifications. Account data can be exported or deleted in your profile.

Check history is retained for up to 90 days. Notification history is retained for 30 days after processing. Daily local backups are kept for up to 14 copies. Account deletion removes active profile data and owned monitors. Existing backups expire on their rotation schedule.

Use public URLs. Avoid including private keys, personal identifiers or credentials in addresses submitted for monitoring.

Terms

Use monitoring for sites you own or have permission to monitor. Secura GE reports observations from the configured checks. Timing and visibility depend on check intervals, reachable networks and the chosen notification channels.
